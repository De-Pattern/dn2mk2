DN2 mk2 MOD

The manual · covers v0.67 MIDIARP

The DN2 mk2 mod is unofficial firmware for the Elektron Digitone II on OS 1.11. It adds the Machinedrum's machines (PERC), eight Monomachine machines, the Digitakt II's sample machines with a sample library on the +Drive, FLEX live buffers, a recorder, Direct Jump, parameter slides, keyboard HOLD and microtuning. Everything stock still works as in Elektron's manual; this page covers only what the mod adds. Not made or supported by Elektron.

The version you're running shows as "MOD v…" at the top of the sample browser and over the last frame of the start-up animation.

Install & recovery

  • Stock OS 1.11 only. The mod is built on 1.11 (so there's no Outbox). On 1.12 or later, flashing it takes you back to a 1.11-based OS.
  • Back up first, every time: the +Drive and your projects, with Elektron Transfer.
  • Build the file in the DN2 Firmware Patcher from your own stock files (the Digitone II 1.11, the Digitakt II, the Machinedrum and the Monomachine OS files). It only lets you save a file that is byte for byte the cleared build. Flash it over USB like any OS update, at low volume.
  • Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery (the patcher's Flash section). The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
  • Each build contains everything before it: flash only the newest.

PERC: the Machinedrum

34 Machinedrum machines in one, run from your MD's own firmware: TRX (BD B2 SD XT CP RS CB CH OH CY MA CL XC S2), EFM (BD SD XT CP RS CB HH CY), GND (SN NS IM) and the physical-model P-I machines (BD SD MT ML MA RS RC CC HH). The MD's sample-based E-12 machines are not included.

  • Page 1: the chosen machine's eight parameters, named as on the MD. The labels follow TYPE.
  • Page 2: TYPE picks the machine, then the MD's track effects: AMD, AMF (amp modulation), EQF, EQG (EQ), FLTF, FLTW, FLTQ (filter).
  • TYPE can be p-locked, so each step of a PERC track can be a different machine.
  • Hold SYN to see the values. FUNC + turn on TYPE steps one machine a click.

Monomachine machines

Built from your Monomachine SFX-6/60 OS file. Polyphonic: each note is its own voice, as many as DSP time allows (see DSP time). Each page is drawn the Monomachine's way, with its own animated controls; choice knobs take four detents a choice on a short list.

  • Shape them with the AMP envelope. As on the Monomachine, these voices never go quiet on their own.
  • TUNE (knob H) reads −64..+63, 0 in tune, the whole knob ±2 semitones. FUNC + turn jumps a semitone.
  • FM+ DYN's 1FRQ and 2FRQ are continuous; FUNC + turn jumps to whole ratios (v0.66): 1FRQ 0, 1, 2; 2FRQ 0, 1, 2, 3, 4.

Controls

  • SAMP runs through the project's samples in four banks (OFF, A001..A127, B001..B127, C001..C127, D001..D127; v0.65), then the FLEX buffers B1..B8 (shown with the bank letter F). SAMP's box shows the bank letter. A track on Bn plays that buffer's current take. Turning SAMP opens the sample list (see Samples & +Drive).
  • STRT, LEN, LOOP are the DT2's fine controls, 0.00..120.00. Turn for fine steps, push and turn for whole steps. Turned while a note plays, the change takes effect at the next trig or loop pass.
  • TUNE plays its fine values (a detent is 0.01; FUNC + turn is 12.00). It is drawn as WaveTone's ruler.
  • PLAY: FWD, FWD loop, REV, REV loop, two detents a mode. WERP's MODE, BARS and GRID take four detents a choice.
  • A turned control shows its value under it for a second. Hold SYN to see every value.
  • SAMP and SLCE can be p-locked and LFO-modulated: an LFO on SAMP steps through your loaded samples.
  • MACHINE SEL from one sample machine to another keeps SAMP.

  • SLICE plays up to 64 slices: the WAV's cue points, a grid, or detected hits. A sample with no slices plays whole.
  • SLCE at NOTE: the note picks the slice, counted from C1 as on the DT2. In KEYBOARD mode each key plays its own slice; KEYBOARD + UP/DOWN moves on by the next set of slices.
  • YES on the page opens the menu:
    • EDIT SLICE POINTS: the slice editor (below).
    • CREATE SLICE GRID: SLICES 2..64 (FUNC + E jumps 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64) or TR.DET (transient detection). LINKED / UNLINKED start and end points (knob A). Then on to the editor.
    • CLEAR SLICE GRID: removes the sample's slices.
    • GRID's ALLOCATE SLICE (LINEAR / RANDOM): locks a slice on each note trig. NO leaves the menu keeping the locks.

The slice editor

  • E moves START, H moves END; FUNC + E or H snaps to the nearest zero crossing. G moves the view; FUNC + G moves the whole slice, keeping its length.
  • ZOOM goes down to 128 frames.
  • FUNC + RIGHT splits the slice at its middle; FUNC + LEFT removes it (the audio stays).
  • FUNC + YES plays the slice while held. D sets the preview to once or looping.
  • Edits play live: a slice's key plays the change 0.2 s after the knobs stop. Playing a slice (keys, trigs, sequencer) selects it.
  • YES or NO closes the editor.

FLEX: live buffers

FLEX records a track, the input or the main mix into one of eight RAM buffers on sequencer trigs, and plays the take back like ONESHOT, the Octatrack way.

  • BUF picks the buffer the track records into and plays, four detents a buffer. A trig with BUF p-locked plays that buffer.
  • Choosing FLEX in MACHINE SEL sets the track's trig LEN to INF, so a trig plays the whole take.
  • Any sample machine can play a buffer too: SAMP past 127 to B1..B8.
  • The buffers live in RAM: they're gone at power-off. Save a take to keep it (FUNC + YES, below).

  • YES records by hand (at the next Q.REC boundary when set; NO cancels the wait).
  • A trig that arrives while a take is recording is refused (BUSY); it never cuts the take.
  • The left column shows the buffer's state (EMPTY, READY, REC, WAIT, BUSY), the take's length and a level meter (CLIP at full scale).
  • MUTE SRC: with SRC on a track and MUTE ON, that track is taken out of the mix (its level and FX sends) only while the FLEX track plays its take. FLEX still records it at full level. Stopping or muting the FLEX track, or MUTE OFF, gives it back. Not stock mute: the track's LED doesn't change.
  • FUNC + NO: CLEAR BUF n? (YES empties the buffer).
  • FUNC + YES saves the take as a sample (TRIM, FILE NAME, ASSIGN), exactly as recorded (never normalised).
  • The settings (SRC, CHAN, R.LEN, GAIN, Q.REC, ARM, MUTE) are the FLEX track's own, saved with the kit.
Level. A take of a single track plays at its source's level with GAIN at 0 (v0.65; on v0.63 and v0.64 it was about 12 dB too loud). A take of MAIN or IN is recorded as it comes in and played 12 dB up, on FLEX and on any sample machine playing the buffer, so it can't clip (v0.66). Saved as a sample, a MAIN or IN take plays 12 dB quieter than it did live.

  • Then, as on the DT2: TRIM (with preview; F zooms 2, 4, 8 times, G moves along), FILE NAME (the Digitone's own naming screen, up to 35 characters), ASSIGN (press a [TRIG] to put the sample on that track, NO skips).

  • R.LEN counts steps at the pattern's scale.

Samples & the +Drive

There are two stores, as on the Digitakt II: PROJECT RAM (the samples the project holds, 400 MB, what the tracks play) and +DRIVE (the library, about 20 GB, in folders).

The sample list

  • Turning SAMP opens it: OFF, the slots bank by bank (A..D, 127 each), then the FLEX buffers B1..B8. UP/DOWN move, held they scroll; FUNC + UP/DOWN jumps a whole bank.
  • FUNC + YES opens the browser.

The browser

  • PROJECT RAM: YES ticks samples. RIGHT acts: LOAD TRK, UNLOAD, RENAME, SELECT ALL, SEL UNUSED (ticks every sample no sound and no p-lock uses).
  • +DRIVE: folders (INCOMING, RECORDED and yours; folders inside folders, six levels). YES ticks; FUNC + YES on a folder ticks everything in it. RIGHT opens SAMPLE ACTION: LOAD TO PROJECT (into the first empty slots of the bank your track's SAMP is on, then the banks after it), LOAD TO TRK (and puts the track's SAMP on it), RENAME, DELETE, MOVE SELECTED / MOVE HERE, NEW FOLDER, SELECT ALL / CLEAR ALL. A sample already in the project isn't loaded twice.
  • Preview: hold FUNC + YES on a sample (its first ten seconds).
  • Keys: NO goes up a level and closes at the top; NO held goes to the top; a page key closes. Held UP/DOWN repeat, FUNC + UP/DOWN a page; the knob pushed and turned moves a page a detent.
  • Long names show head…tail; the selected row scrolls.
  • Names (RENAME, NEW FOLDER, FILE NAME) open the Digitone's own NAME screen, with its edit grid, random name and copy/paste.

Banks (v0.65)

  • Four banks of project RAM, A..D, 127 slots each (508). Banks are slots, not more memory: the 400 MB pool is shared by all four.
  • p-locks, sounds and kits keep the bank (it's stored in SAMP's own value); an LFO on SAMP moves through slots but never into another bank; a MIDI CC on SAMP stays in the bank SAMP is on.
  • Every project made before v0.65 is bank A and plays as before. Each project keeps its own banks.
  • The recorder records into bank A, and the import page sends into bank A; fill banks B..D from the +Drive on the unit.
  • If more than about 31 MB of recordings are kept, banks B..D stay off until the recording at the end of that area is deleted.

Per-project pools

  • Each of the 128 projects keeps its own list of samples. NEW starts empty. Switching projects loads that project's samples; a track stays silent until its own sample is in, never playing another's.
  • At power-on the samples the kit's tracks use load first, so the pattern can play while the rest load (the waveform shows LOADING n%).

Getting samples in

  • DN2 Sample Import (in the browser, over USB) sends WAVs to the +Drive library, into folders (nested paths too), and can list and delete what's there. Nothing in a project changes until you LOAD TO PROJECT.
  • The library holds 16,384 samples and 128 folders (every level of a path counts as one folder).

Sequencer

Direct Jump

Hold PTN and press YES (on) or NO (off); the title bar says which. With it on, a pattern cued with PTN + [TRIG] while playing switches at the next step and carries on from the same position (each track's, wrapped by its new length). Chains, songs and program changes keep stock's change at the end. The setting is kept through power-off.

Parameter slides

A trig with SLIDE glides every continuous parameter its p-locks change, from where the track was to the locked value, instead of jumping (Analog Four / Overtone style).

  • TRIG page 2: SLD on encoder E (ON / OFF), SLEN on encoder F (NXT, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 32, 64 steps, four detents a choice). Hold a trig (in GRID RECORDING, as for any p-lock) and turn. Push E or F with the trig held to clear it.
  • NXT (v0.66; also what a slide trig gets with no SLEN set): the slide lasts until the track's next trig, as on the Analog Four. A note trig or a lock (trigless) trig both count, wrapping round the track's length; with no other trig, the whole length. Chained trigless trigs each slide to the next. Micro timing and trig conditions are not counted. (Until v0.65 a slide with no SLEN lasted 1 step.)
  • Steps follow the track's scale and the tempo. LFOs ride on top. A trig with SLIDE but no p-lock slides nothing.
  • What slides: filter, envelopes, levels, pan, FX sends, LFO speed/depth, the FM machines' and WaveTone/Swarmer's continuous controls, the sample machines' TUNE, LEV, STRT, LEN, LOOP, PERC's track effects and the MD machine controls that change mid-note. Choices (waves, modes, SAMP, TYPE) never slide.
  • Slides are saved with the pattern and travel with copy, paste and undo. On stock firmware they're simply dropped.

Keyboard HOLD

Hold PTN and press KEYBOARD: KB HOLD ON (again: OFF). It works like a sustain pedal held down on every track.

  • Notes keep sounding after you let go of the key or a MIDI note-off arrives. Notes you were already holding when HOLD came on are latched.
  • New notes are added to the held ones. Works with the [TRIG] keys, MIDI in, chords, microtuning and the arpeggiator (the arp keeps running on the latched keys).
  • HOLD off releases everything. It starts off after a power cycle. PTN + KEYBOARD no longer toggles KEYBOARD mode.

The arpeggiator on MIDI tracks (v0.67)

  • The ARPEGGIATOR menu opens on a MIDI track as it does on a synth track.
  • With the arp on, the track's notes (sequencer trigs and keys) go through the arp, and each arp step is sent over MIDI on the track's channel. Its length is the arp's N.LEN (INF is sent as the longest finite length). SPD, RNG, the offsets and velocity work as on a synth track.
  • Arp off: the MIDI track sends as stock. Synth tracks are unchanged.
  • Upstream's work (Angel Linares's dn2_firmware_explore), tested on his unit. HOLD with the arp on a MIDI track hasn't been tried yet.

Microtuning

One tuning per project: 12 offsets (tenths of a cent), one per semitone above its ROOT, repeating every octave. Each track is tuned or not.

  • Factory: 12-TET, Just Major, Just Minor, Pythagorean, Meantone 1/4, Werckmeister III, Kirnberger III, Vallotti, Arabic Rast, Poole Rod12, Poole12 Min. Plus USER 1..8.
  • KB SETUP (FUNC + KEYBOARD), knob H TUNE: OFF or a tuning for the active track. Push H for TUNING: A TUNE, B ROOT, C TRK (this track on/off), D ALL (every track). YES on a USER tuning opens EDIT: A NOTE (or LEFT/RIGHT), B CENT, C .1CT, D FROM; FUNC + YES copies FROM into the slot (copy 12-TET to reset). NO goes back.
  • Everything on a tuned track is retuned: keyboard, trigs, chords, arp, slides. MIDI tracks and MIDI out stay 12-TET. Stock's SCALE, ROOT and MODE still work as stock.
  • Saved with the project (two seconds after the last change).

DSP time

  • The Machinedrum and Monomachine machines are heavy. Voices are shared by every track; when many sound at once the DSP can run out of time. A guard measures each frame: a voice that won't fit rests until its next note instead of breaking up the sound. Sample voices get their time first.
  • Load meter: hold FUNC on a PERC or Monomachine page; the tempo's place shows the DSP load (the last half second's peak). CPU shows there while a voice is being left out for time.
  • Finished voices rest and cost nothing until their next note.

Going back to stock

  • The mod's samples and data live in their own regions of the +Drive. Stock's FORMAT +DRIVE does not remove them.
  • ERASE MOD DATA: at the top of the sample browser, on +DRIVE press RIGHT. It asks twice, shows its progress, and can be paused and resumed. It erases only the mod's regions (pool, pool lists, settings, library).
  • Overbridge: use 2.26.10 or later (the first for OS 1.11).
